# 05 · Signature

## Hypothesis
Concepts 01–04 read as AI-made because each started from a style adjective. This
one starts from the material and holds itself to the craft bar of acoustics
specialists (Kirei, Autex, BuzziSpace) and design-led material brands (Kvadrat,
Mutina, Caesarstone): no metaphor, strong photography, real finishes, and a
sample flow that works on every page.

## Primary audience
Architects and interior designers in early design, shortlisting finishes and
building a sample box. Returning specifiers must still reach NRC, fire class
and CAD files in one or two clicks.

## Key UX ideas
- **Silk Metal leads.** The home page opens on it: installed photo, specs,
  and its 21 finishes, each one a sample you can add in place. It is listed
  first in the navigation and the default product order.
- **Brand palette dominant.** Black, white and deep green carry structure and
  actions; teal is the accent; finish color lives only in swatches and photos.
- **Finish first.** The shared finish wall on home; real finish photos in the
  product finish picker; every sample is a product + finish pair.
- **The sample box.** Eight slots on the samples page, each showing the finish,
  product, NRC, fire class and links to that product's documents.

## What to evaluate
1. Does it still read as AI-made next to 01–04?
2. Is Silk Metal unmistakably the signature without overclaiming?
3. From the home page, how fast can a specifier add three finishes and get a data sheet?

## Direction contract
- **THESIS:** The category standard, played straight at the level of the best
  acoustics and material brands; the one idea it owns is Silk Metal as the
  signature, opening the site with its real finishes as sample actions. It
  refuses the hero-plus-three-cards template, eyebrows, stat strips and any
  borrowed metaphor.
- **OWN-WORLD:** White ground, black type, deep green (#00574F) for every
  action and the footer band, teal (#2B8F92) as a rule and focus accent
  only, Inter Tight at two weights; square photography edge to edge, thin
  black rules, 4px radii on controls only; finish color appears only inside
  swatches and photos.
- **STORY:** The visitor sees Silk Metal installed, understands what it is and
  how it performs, adds a finish or two, then moves through the range, the
  finish wall and the documents, and leaves with a sample request.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed installed Silk Metal photograph across the top
  60%; below it, on white, "Silk Metal" at display size left, "Micro-perforated
  aluminum" and one sentence; to the right a spec row (NRC range, Class A,
  21 finishes) and the 21 finish chips, each an add-sample button, with
  "See all 7 Silk Metal systems" as the primary action. Header: wordmark,
  Products, Projects, Resources, Samples (count).
- **FORM:** Category standard (canon), chosen by the user over the roll; seed key af4b814f.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
