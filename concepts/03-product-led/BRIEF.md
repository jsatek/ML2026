# 03 · Product-led

## Hypothesis
Most visitors arrive with a job to do: "I need a Class A ceiling baffle in a warm gray
with NRC above 0.8." A site that behaves like a capable product tool, with fast
filtering, instant feedback, and fewer pages to click through, converts more specifiers
into sample requests and downloads than a brochure does.

## Primary audience
Specifiers, designers, and contractors mid-project who know their requirements.

## Key UX ideas
- **Global search** in the header covers products and all 277 documents, with instant results.
- **Guided finder on the home page:** surface → type → material → performance, with a live result count and results updating as you choose.
- **Faceted explorer:** live counts on every facet, an NRC minimum slider, Class A toggle, **shop by color** (finishes grouped into color families), removable active-filter chips, grid/list views, and side-by-side compare.
- **Configurator product page:** choose a design option and a finish (with texture previews), then a summary panel to add a sample or grab the spec kit; specs, documents, and projects in tabs.
- **Document picker:** choose products and document types, check the files you need, then copy links or export a CSV. A "spec kit" in two clicks.
- **Sample drawer:** the cart slides in from any page, so browsing never stops.

## What to evaluate
1. Time to find a product that meets a stated requirement (e.g. "Class A wall panel, NRC ≥ 0.8, dark green").
2. Do live facet counts and color search reduce dead ends?
3. Is the configurator clear about what a sample includes (finish) vs. what's for reference (design option)?
